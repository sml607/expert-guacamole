How to redeem safely

A simple checklist before you use any gift card.

STEP 01

Check the card

Make sure the card is for the service and country/region you intend to use.

STEP 02

Open the official site

Use the official link above or the company's official app. Check the website address carefully.

STEP 03

Follow its instructions

Sign in to your own account and enter the code only on the official service's redemption screen.

STEP 04

Keep your receipt

Save the purchase receipt. If a code does not work, contact the card issuer through its official support.

Common questions

Does SML GIFT CARD redeem cards for me?

No. It is an information website that links to official redemption instructions. It does not process, buy, exchange or cash out gift cards.

Should I send my card code to someone?

No. Treat the code like cash. Anyone who gets it may be able to spend the balance. Never share it in chat, email, calls or social media.

What if my card's country does not match my account?

Do not try unofficial workarounds. Check the issuer's country and region rules or contact its official support.

Is SML affiliated with Apple, Google, Amazon, Steam, PlayStation or Microsoft?

No. These are independent third-party services. Their names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.
